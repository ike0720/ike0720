# Truck Power Distribution System

## Overview
Designed and installed a custom 12V power distribution system in my truck for camping using a 100Ah LiFePO4 battery, 2000W inverter, junction box, battery isolating switch, USB-C ports, 12V Switch Pannel, 12V LED strip lights, LED pod lights, and a light bar.

The goal was to create a reliable off-grid power system for lighting, charging, and accessory use while keeping the installation clean, safe, and serviceable.

## System Features
- 100Ah 12V LiFePO4 battery
- 2000W inverter for AC power
- Master isolating switch
- Junction box for organized power distribution
- USB-C charging ports
- 12V LED strip lights
- LED pod lights
- Light bar
- Hidden wiring and clean install

## System Design

### Power Architecture
- Battery feeds the main isolating switch
- Power is distributed through a junction box
- Inverter is connected for high-power AC loads
- Separate circuits support lighting and accessory outputs

### Wiring and Protection
- Wiring routed to reduce clutter and avoid pinch points
- Connections protected against vibration and movement
- Exposed wiring protected from moisture where needed
- Components positioned for both function and accessibility

## Challenges

### Installation and Space Constraints
Working within the limited space of a vehicle made component placement and wiring routing difficult, especially while maintaining accessibility and serviceability.

**Solution:**
- Planned the component layout before installation
- Mounted the battery, inverter, and junction box in compact, accessible locations
- Routed wiring efficiently to minimize clutter and improve serviceability

### Wire Management and Concealment
Running multiple power lines for lights, charging ports, and accessories created challenges in keeping the wiring clean, hidden, and protected.

**Solution:**
- Routed wiring along factory paths where possible
- Used loom and protective sleeving to organize and conceal wires
- Secured wiring to prevent movement, rattling, and wear over time

### Waterproofing and Environmental Protection
Vehicle environments expose components to moisture, dirt, vibration, and temperature changes.

**Solution:**
- Used sealed connections and heat shrink for exposed wiring
- Positioned sensitive components away from potential water exposure
- Protected wiring routes to reduce environmental wear

### Component Positioning and Usability
Proper placement of lights, switches, and power outputs was important for usability and performance.

**Solution:**
- Positioned lighting for useful coverage and visibility
- Placed switches and power outputs in accessible locations
- Balanced functionality with a clean, hidden installation

## Results
The final system successfully:
- Provides reliable off-grid power for truck accessories
- Supports lighting, charging, and AC power needs
- Keeps wiring clean, hidden, and protected
- Allows the system to be isolated when not in use
- Improves usability for camping, working, and nighttime setup
