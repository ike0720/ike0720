# Testing Plan

## Bench Testing

- Assemble the printed mechanism with the real pivot hardware.
- Move the arm by hand through the full travel.
- Confirm the arm reaches the raised and lowered positions without binding.
- Check washer spacing and bushing contact.
- Tighten the clamp bolts gradually and watch for printed part deformation.
- Mark any rubbing, cracking, or flexing areas.

## Servo Fit Test

- Mount the servo without powering it.
- Move the arm by hand and confirm linkage clearance.
- Check that the linkage does not go over-center.
- Confirm that the servo horn does not hit the housing.
- Make sure the servo is not used as the physical stop.

## Powered Test

- Start with conservative servo angles.
- Move in small steps from lowered to raised.
- Stop before the physical 90 degree stop.
- Increase travel only after confirming there is no binding.
- Run at least 20 open/close cycles on the bench.

## Boat Fit Test

- Install the clamp with a rubber or neoprene liner.
- Tighten evenly across the clamp bolts.
- Pull gently on the flag arm to simulate wind load.
- Check whether the clamp rotates, slides, or marks the mounting surface.
- Recheck fasteners after the first few minutes of use.

## Pass Criteria

- Clamp stays in place under hand-applied load.
- Pivot rotates smoothly without visible wear after repeated cycles.
- Servo moves the arm without buzzing or stalling.
- Printed parts show no cracks around pivot, stop, or clamp bolts.
- The mechanism can be removed without damaging the boat mounting surface.
