# Bill of Materials

This BOM is written for a low-cost prototype. Prices are rough estimates and should be updated after parts are purchased.

| Item | Qty | Estimated Cost | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Printed clamp body | 1 set | $0-$10 | PLA prototype, PETG or PA12 later if needed |
| Printed rotating flag arm | 1 | $0-$5 | Print solid enough around pivot and stop areas |
| Waterproof metal-gear servo | 1 | $12-$20 | Target 25 kg-cm to 35 kg-cm |
| Stainless pivot bolt or shoulder bolt | 1 | $2-$6 | Main structural pivot member |
| Stainless washers | Several | $2-$5 | Use on both ends and between moving faces |
| Locknut | 1 | $1-$3 | Prevents pivot from loosening |
| Clamp bolts | 6 | $3-$8 | Stainless preferred |
| Clamp nuts or heat-set inserts | 6 | $3-$8 | Avoid relying on plastic threads |
| Bushing material | 1 set | $0-$5 | Bronze, nylon, or thin aluminum prototype bushing |
| Rubber or neoprene liner | 1 | $0-$5 | Old bicycle tube can work |
| Microcontroller | 1 | $0-$10 | Arduino or ESP32 if not already available |
| Fuse holder and fuse | 1 | $3-$8 | Recommended for boat power |

## Budget Notes

The project target is about $40 for a working prototype. That is possible if the printed parts are made at home and some hardware is already available.

The best places to spend money are:

1. Servo
2. Pivot hardware
3. Washers/bushings
4. Stainless clamp hardware

The worst place to spend money early is full CNC aluminum for every part before the geometry and servo motion are proven.
